The Village Oven is Broken!

I need your help to bake Christmas cookies! Here is a simple recipe:

Simple Butter Cookies:
  • 250g Flour
  • 125g Sugar
  • 125g Butter
  • 1 Egg
  • 1 tsp Vanilla Sugar

Mix ingredients, chill dough for 30 min, roll out, cut shapes, and bake at 180°C (350°F) for 10-12 mins.
